Arrow Climb Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

In short: Arrow Climb doesn't ask for your name, email or any account. Your progress, coins and settings stay on your phone. To show ads and to find and fix bugs, the game uses Google services (Google AdMob and Google Firebase). They collect some information about your device and how the game is used, as explained below. We never sell your information.

Who we are

Arrow Climb (the Android game com.arrowclimb.game) is made by Rimeso ("we", "us"). For any question about this policy or your data, write to rajrock7254@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your game progress (levels unlocked, best results), coins, the looks you bought and your settings are saved only on your phone. They are not sent to us. They are deleted when you uninstall the game, or when you choose Reset progress in the game's settings.

What the Google services collect

Ads: Google AdMob

The game shows ads: an optional video to double your coins, an optional video to carry on after a fall, and a short ad between some levels. To choose, show and measure ads and to prevent ad fraud, Google AdMob collects:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which gives an approximate location (city or region, not your exact position);
  • information about your device and the app (such as model, operating system, language);
  • how you interact with ads (views, taps, finished videos).

Whether ads are personalised depends on your consent (in the regions where it is asked) and on your device's ad settings.

Game statistics: Google Analytics for Firebase

To learn which levels are too hard and how to make the game better, we use Google Analytics for Firebase. It collects:

  • a random app instance ID (it doesn't tell us who you are);
  • game events such as levels started and finished, falls and what caused them, coins earned and spent, the screens you visit, and when you watch an optional video;
  • device information (model, operating system, language) and an approximate location worked out from your IP address;
  • your device's advertising ID.

Crash reports: Firebase Crashlytics

If the game crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics sends us a report so we can fix it: what went wrong in the code, the state of the device at that moment (such as model, operating system, free memory), a random installation ID, and the last few game events before the problem.

How we use it

  • to show ads, which keep the game free to play;
  • to understand how the game is played and improve levels and difficulty;
  • to find and fix crashes and bugs;
  • to prevent fraud and abuse.

We don't use the information to find out who you are, and we never sell it.

Who receives it

The information above goes to Google, which runs AdMob and Firebase. Google may process it on servers in different countries. Google uses it under its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Privacy and security in Firebase

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent to use your data for ads the first time you open it. You can change your choice at any time from the game's settings.
  • Ad personalisation: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Optional videos: the reward videos only play when you tap to watch one.
  • Deleting: uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your phone. To ask us to delete data held by the services above, email us; we will do what we can, although the data is not linked to your name.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe, or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to how it is used. Email us and we will answer within 30 days.

Children

Arrow Climb is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Security

All information sent by the game to Google's services is encrypted on its way (TLS / HTTPS). No method is perfect, but we only use well-established services and collect as little as we can.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top. Big changes will also be mentioned in the game's update notes.
