Arrow Climb: Stickman Parkour

Their arrows. Your steps.

Get it on Google Play

The game

Enemy archers shoot arrows into the wall to stop you, and every arrow that sticks becomes your next step. Wait for it, jump onto it, climb higher and reach the flag.

  • 20 hand-made adventures: an old well, a runaway mine cart, balloons above the clouds, a hot-air balloon over a stormy river, city rooftops with a zipline and a lift.
  • Jump, duck, dodge: spear guards, hunters, rolling boulders and swooping crows.
  • 5 worlds and endless levels after the hand-made ones.
  • Coins, hats and scarves, and four tough challenge levels.
  • One-thumb controls, checkpoints everywhere, three difficulties, and it plays offline.
