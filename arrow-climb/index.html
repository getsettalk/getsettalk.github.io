About the game

Enemy archers shoot arrows into the wall to stop you, and every arrow that sticks becomes your next step. Wait for it, jump onto it, climb higher and reach the flag.

Every level is a new journey. Fall down an old well, race a mine cart through the dark, bounce on balloons above the clouds, steer a hot-air balloon between rocks and storm clouds, and zip across city rooftops.

Features

  • 200+ levels across 5 worlds, with more on the way
  • Wells, mine carts, balloons, hot-air balloons, ziplines and lifts
  • Guards, hunters, boulders and crows to jump, duck and dodge
  • Coins to spend on hats, scarves and challenge levels
  • One-thumb controls and checkpoints everywhere
  • Small download, plays offline
